Port Solano is a complete top-down open-world crime game in the browser, all original: a sun-soaked coastal city of 64 blocks with named buildings, palms, a plaza and a beach, generated from a seed. Play Reyes, a courier paying off a debt to marina owner Sol Vega, through the five-job story "Solano Nights" — deliveries against the clock, a ledger car to steal from the courthouse, a harbour run under police heat, and a final drive that has to end clean — then keep the city as a free-roam sandbox with repeatable club work. Traffic keeps to its lanes and turns at intersections, pedestrians stroll and flee, any car can be stolen and driven, crimes raise a five-star wanted level only when seen, police steer in pursuit and give up when you stay out of sight, and there is shooting, car damage, a HUD with a live minimap, synthesized engine and siren sound, and a save in localStorage. Static files, no build step, no libraries, fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
